Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Quant Trade Analyzer (the QuantTrade app, including the web app at quant-trade-neon.vercel.app and the Android app) does and does not collect, and the third-party services it relies on. Effective date: November 4, 2026.

// 01 · ABOUT THE APP

What Quant Trade Analyzer is.

Quant Trade Analyzer provides stock price forecasting using Monte Carlo simulation and market analysis generated with the Google Gemini large language model. It draws market data from third-party financial data APIs and company filings from SEC EDGAR, the public filings database of the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission.

No user account or registration is required to view forecasts. The app does not execute trades, does not connect to brokerage accounts, and does not ask for financial account credentials of any kind.

⚠ NOT FINANCIAL ADVICE

Quant Trade Analyzer is an educational and informational tool only. Its forecasts are statistical simulations based on historical and publicly available data; they are not predictions of future performance and are not financial, investment, or trading advice.

Nothing in the app is a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any security. Always do your own research and consider consulting a licensed financial advisor before making investment decisions. The developer is not responsible for losses arising from decisions made using this app.

// 02 · INFORMATION WE COLLECT

Very little — by design.

Because no sign-up is required, the app does not build user accounts or profiles. Specifically:

  • Information you provide: none is required to use the app. If you contact the developer by email, your email address and the contents of your message are used only to reply to you.
  • Lookups you make: when you view a forecast, the stock symbol you request is sent to our backend and to data providers so the forecast can be generated. Symbols are market data, not personal information.
  • Technical information: the hosting provider may process standard technical data (such as IP address, browser or device type, and request timestamps) in server logs, as is normal for any website or app backend. See the Analytics section below.
  • Advertising identifiers (Android app): if the Android app displays advertising served by Google AdMob, AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising identifier to serve and measure ads. See the Third-Party Services section below.

We do not sell personal information. We do not collect brokerage credentials, payment card details, or government identification, and we never ask for them.

// 03 · HOW INFORMATION IS USED

To run the app, nothing more.

The limited information described above is used only to operate and improve the app: generating the forecasts you request, keeping the service secure and reliable, understanding aggregate usage so features can be improved, and — in the Android app, if advertising is enabled — serving ads through Google AdMob. It is not used to build advertising profiles of our own, and it is not sold or rented to third parties.

// 04 · THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

Services the app relies on.

Generating a forecast requires data and infrastructure from third parties. Each provider handles data under its own privacy policy:

  • Google Gemini — market analysis is generated using Google's Gemini large language model. The market data and context needed to produce an analysis are sent to Google's API for processing.
  • Financial data providers — stock prices and related market data come from third-party financial data APIs. Your requested stock symbols are sent to these providers to retrieve that data.
  • SEC EDGAR — company filings are retrieved from EDGAR, the U.S. SEC's public database of company filings. EDGAR data is public information.
  • Vercel — the web app and backend are hosted on Vercel, which processes standard technical request data to serve the site.
  • Google AdMob (Android app) — the Android app may display advertising served by Google AdMob. When it does, AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier and related device data to serve and measure ads, in line with Google's advertising policies. You can reset or delete your advertising identifier, and opt out of personalised advertising, in your Android device settings.

// 05 · ANALYTICS

Aggregate usage, not people.

We may use privacy-respecting analytics on the web app (such as Vercel Analytics) to understand aggregate usage — for example, how many forecasts are generated and which features are used — so the app can be improved. These analytics are used in aggregate form and are not used to identify individual users or to track you across other sites or apps for advertising.

// 06 · DATA RETENTION & SECURITY

Kept briefly, if at all.

Because there are no user accounts, we do not retain personal profiles or histories of your activity. Cached market and filing data is kept so the app can respond quickly, and routine server logs are retained by the hosting provider for a limited period for security and reliability, after which they are deleted or anonymised. Email you send us is kept only as long as needed to respond and maintain reasonable records of the correspondence. We use reasonable technical measures to protect the limited data involved, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

// 07 · CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Not directed at children.

Quant Trade Analyzer is a financial information tool intended for a general adult audience. It is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the app (for example, by emailing us), please contact us and we will delete it.

// 08 · CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

If this policy changes.

We may update this policy from time to time — for example, if features or third-party services change. When we do, the updated policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Material changes will be reflected here before or at the time they take effect, and continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
